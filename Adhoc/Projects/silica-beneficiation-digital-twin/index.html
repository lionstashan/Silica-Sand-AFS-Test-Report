    CONCEPTUAL · NOT FOR CONSTRUCTION

    Design basis and assumptions

    Feed

    50 t/h dry silica sand, 8% as-received moisture, nominal top size 10 mm, bulk density 1.6 t/m³ and particle density 2.65 t/m³.

    Products

    One washed and dewatered silica product, nominal 0.1–1.0 mm. Base-case mass recovery is 85%; product moisture is assumed at 12%.

    Process route

    Feed hopper → wet scalping screen → rotary scrubber → attrition cells → cyclone classification → wet magnetic separator → dewatering screen.

    Water circuit

    Thickener and filter press return clarified water. Base circulation is 2.5 m³ per tonne of dry feed with 90% recycle.

    Power

    Operating demand is estimated from utilization factors. Pump shaft power uses flow, total dynamic head, slurry density and efficiency; motor ratings are rounded up to a standard size.

    Required validation

    Representative sieve and chemical analysis, scrubbing tests, attrition tests, magnetic response, settling tests, pump curves, site elevations and final pipe routing.

    Engineering limitation: This model is for layout, discussion and early feasibility. Do not purchase equipment or construct the plant from these values without laboratory testing and review by qualified mineral-processing, mechanical, electrical and civil engineers.
