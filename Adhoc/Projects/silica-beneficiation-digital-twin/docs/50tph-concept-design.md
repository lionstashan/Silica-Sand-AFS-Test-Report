# 50 TPH Rajasthan Silica Beneficiation Concept

This is a deliberately generic wet-beneficiation plant for discussion and simulation. It is not tied to a specific Rajasthan orebody, mine, vendor, or final product specification.

## Base case

- Dry feed: 50 t/h, with 65 t/h short-duration mechanical design allowance.
- Feed: 94% SiO2, 8% surface moisture, nominally below 10 mm after mining/crushing.
- Dry product: 42.5 t/h at 85% mass recovery.
- Indicative product: approximately 98.7% SiO2 based on an assumed 89.3% recovery of silica mineral.
- Process water circulation: 125 m3/h at 2.5 m3/t dry feed.
- Water recycle: 90%; theoretical make-up 12.5 m3/h before detailed losses.
- Connected motor load: approximately 225 kW, excluding buildings, laboratory and future equipment.

## Flow route

1. Grizzly hopper and belt feeder regulate pre-crushed feed.
2. Wet scalping screen rejects oversize.
3. Rotary scrubber disperses clay coatings and agglomerates.
4. Attrition cells clean grain surfaces.
5. Slurry pump and hydrocyclones remove fine slimes.
6. Wet high-intensity magnetic separator removes responsive iron contaminants.
7. Dewatering screen produces conveyable washed sand.
8. Thickener returns clarified water; filter press dewaters the sludge.

## Calculation boundaries

Pump power is `shaft kW = density × 9.81 × flow(m3/s) × head(m) / (efficiency × 1000)`. The motor is the next standard rating above shaft power plus 15% preliminary margin. Final selection requires vendor curves, slurry correction, NPSH, pipe losses and site elevations.

Mechanical drives are preliminary installed ratings. Rotation speeds are starting ranges, not guaranteed optima. Screen motion, attrition intensity, magnetic field, cyclone pressure and scrubber retention must be calibrated with representative material.

## Data needed next

- Full sieve analysis and size-by-size chemistry.
- Fe2O3, Al2O3, TiO2, LOI and clay mineralogy.
- Scrubbing, attrition and magnetic-separation test results.
- Slurry settling and filter-press tests.
- Product specifications, site elevations, pipe routes, water source and electrical standard.
